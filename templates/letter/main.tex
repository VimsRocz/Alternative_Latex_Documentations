% Second Paragraph: Main Content

Be specific and organized. If you are writing a cover letter for a job, highlight

% Third Paragraph: Supporting Details (Optional)

to happen next. For example, request a meeting, express your hope for a response, or 
indicate that you will follow up. Thank the recipient for their time and consideration.
